How much does a new roof cost in Cary, NC?

How long does a roof last in Wake County, NC?

In North Carolina's humid subtropical climate (Zone 4A), standard 3-tab shingles typically last 15 to 18 years, while architectural dimensional shingles last 22 to 30 years.

Do I need a building permit for roof replacement in Cary, NC?

Yes, the Town of Cary Inspections and Permits Department requires a residential building permit when replacing more than 100 square feet of decking or performing a complete roof replacement. Our verified contractors handle all permitting directly.
